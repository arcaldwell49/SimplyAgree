# Articles

### All vignettes

- [Agreement
  Tests](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/agree_tests.md):

  Legacy agreement tests in SimplyAgree (agree_test, agree_reps,
  agree_nest), plus concordance correlation (CCC) and non-parametric
  limits of agreement with agree_np.

- [Agreement & Tolerance
  Limits](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/agreement_analysis.md):

  Bland-Altman limits of agreement and tolerance limits in R with
  agreement_limit() and tolerance_limit(), for simple, replicate, and
  nested data.

- [Agreement for Categorical and Ordinal
  Ratings](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/categorical_agreement.md):

  Agreement for categorical and ordinal ratings in R: kappa, Gwet’s AC1,
  and Krippendorff’s alpha with cac_test(), and model-based kappa for
  many raters with ord_kappa_test().

- [Errors-in-Variables
  Regression](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/Deming.md):

  Errors-in-variables regression for method comparison in R: Deming
  regression with dem_reg() and Passing-Bablok regression with pb_reg().

- [Power Analysis and Sample Size Determination for Agreement
  Studies](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/power_sample_size_vignette.md):

  Power analysis and sample size planning for Bland-Altman agreement
  studies: exact agreement tests, limits of agreement power, expected CI
  half-width, and assurance.

- [Re-analysis of an Agreement
  Study](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/reanalysis.md):

  Worked example re-analysing a published agreement study of rectal
  vs. esophageal temperature with concordance correlation, limits of
  agreement, and tolerance limits.

- [Reliability
  Analysis](https://aaroncaldwell.us/SimplyAgree/articles/reliability_analysis.md):

  Test-retest and inter-rater reliability in R: intraclass correlation
  (ICC) with icc_test(), SEM and coefficient of variation, minimal
  detectable change, and study planning.
